# Audio notes

The demo uses **Tonight** by Michael Ramir C. (Mixkit Stock Music Free License) as its music bed. The 103 BPM hip-hop/electronic pulse was selected for a measured, confident product-launch tone rather than a club or game feel.

- Track: `tonight-hiphop.mp3`
- Source: https://assets.mixkit.co/music/841/841.mp3
- License: Mixkit Stock Music Free License, free commercial use and no attribution required

All sound effects are from the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License collection. Their original names and source URLs are preserved in the video-shotcraft skill’s `assets/audio/ATTRIBUTION.md`; this project uses only the entries with individually recorded URLs. Sound is registered centrally in `src/RhetorixDemo.tsx`, and every cue is expressed relative to its shot start.
