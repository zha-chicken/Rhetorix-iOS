# Rhetorix Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

_Last updated: July 11, 2026_

Rhetorix is a local-first debate training app. This policy describes what the app does — and deliberately does not do — with your information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Rhetorix has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of its own. We never see your data.

## Where your data lives

- **Debate sessions, practice history, learning profile, and settings** are stored as a local file on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **AI provider API keys** you enter are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. They are sent only to the provider they belong to, only to make the requests you trigger.

## What leaves your device

When you use an AI feature (debating, judging, analysis, rebuttal training, safety checks, or online voice playback), the text of that exchange is sent directly from your device to the AI provider **you configured** (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, Groq, or Volcengine). That data is handled under the chosen provider's own privacy policy. Rhetorix does not route this traffic through any intermediate server.

If you do not configure a provider, nothing leaves your device.

## Microphone and speech recognition

Voice input uses the iOS microphone and Apple's speech recognition to transcribe your speeches. Recordings are transcribed and discarded; Rhetorix stores only the resulting text in your local session history. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Children

Rhetorix does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text ships with the app and is published at this page.

## Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/zha-chicken/Rhetorix-iOS/issues.

# 隐私政策（中文）

_更新日期：2026 年 7 月 11 日_

Rhetorix 是一款本地优先的辩论训练应用。

## 我们收集什么

**不收集任何数据。** Rhetorix 没有账号系统、没有分析统计、没有跟踪，也没有自己的服务器。我们不会看到你的任何数据。

## 数据存放在哪里

- **辩论记录、练习历史、学习档案与设置**仅以本地文件形式保存在你的设备上，删除应用即删除数据。
- **你填写的 AI 服务商 API 密钥**保存在设备的 iOS 钥匙串中，仅在你触发请求时发送给对应的服务商。

## 哪些数据会离开设备

当你使用 AI 功能（辩论、评审、分析、反驳训练、内容安全检测或在线语音播报）时，相关文本会从你的设备直接发送给**你自己配置的** AI 服务商（例如 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google、DeepSeek、Groq 或火山引擎），并受该服务商的隐私政策约束。Rhetorix 不经由任何中间服务器转发这些数据。

如果你没有配置任何服务商，数据不会离开设备。

## 麦克风与语音识别

语音输入使用 iOS 麦克风和 Apple 语音识别转写你的发言。录音在转写后即被丢弃，Rhetorix 只在本地历史中保存转写文本。

## 联系方式

请在 https://github.com/zha-chicken/Rhetorix-iOS/issues 提交问题。
